## en-US

Name: CNrecipe

Subtitle: Your Chinese cooking companion

Description:
Explore Chinese recipes and bring your cooking plans together in one place.

Keep track of ingredients in My Kitchen, save recipes for later, and build a shopping list for your next meal. Adjust recipe portions from 1 to 8 servings and choose US or metric units. Follow preparation checklists and cooking steps, use step timers, and save a rating and note when you finish.

Your kitchen, favorites, shopping list, preferences and cooking history are stored on your device. No account is required. Recipe browsing and search use an online catalog; saved recipe content and bundled recipes support offline use. Availability depends on the content already stored on your device.

Notifications are optional and are used for cooking timers. Local data is not an account backup or a cross-device synchronization service.

Recipe guidance does not replace food-safety advice. Review ingredients and allergen information before cooking.

## 审核说明草稿（英文）

CNrecipe does not require an account. The current version stores user kitchen data locally; it does not expose sign-in or account synchronization.

To review the core flow: open Explore, select a recipe, adjust servings and units, open preparation, check the ingredients, and start cooking. Use a timed step to test the optional notification permission. Complete the cooking flow and explicitly save a rating/note to create a history entry. Closing the completion screen alone does not save history.

The public recipe catalog requires network access. Bundled content provides a fallback when available. Timer notifications are optional; the in-app timer remains available if permission is declined.
