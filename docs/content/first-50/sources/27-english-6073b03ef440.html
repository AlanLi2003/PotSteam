Steamed Fish Head with Chopped Chili

Medium⏱ Prep: 20 min🍳 Cook: 15 min👤 4 servings🌶️🌶️🌶️ HotChopped Chili Steaming (剁椒)

📖 The Story

In China, the Bighead Carp's head is more valuable than its body. During Chinese New Year, a single premium fish head can sell for over ¥100 ($15). This dish turns what Americans consider "trash fish" into a delicacy worthy of celebration.

⚠️ Bone & Safety Note

Fish heads contain bones. The cheek meat is the prize — guide your guests to it.

Ingredients

  • •
    1 large (about 2 lbs)Bighead Carp head (or Silver Carp head)— Split in half, cleaned. Ask your fishmonger to do this.

Instructions

1

Clean the fish head thoroughly. Remove gills and any blood. Score the thickest parts with a knife to help flavors penetrate.

2

Marinate with Shaoxing wine, soy sauce, white pepper, and half the ginger for 15 minutes.

3

Spread a layer of scallion whites and remaining ginger on a heatproof plate. Place the fish head skin-side up on top.

4

Generously spread duo jiao (fermented chopped chili) over the entire surface of the fish head. Add minced garlic.

5

Steam over boiling water for 12-15 minutes until the fish is just cooked through (opaque and flaky).

6

Remove from steamer. Top with fresh scallion greens and drizzle sesame oil.

7

Heat vegetable oil until smoking. Pour the hot oil over the scallions for a dramatic sizzle. Serve immediately.

💡 Pro Tip

The key is fresh duo jiao (fermented chopped chili). You can find it at Asian grocery stores or make your own. The fish head should be halved and cleaned thoroughly.
