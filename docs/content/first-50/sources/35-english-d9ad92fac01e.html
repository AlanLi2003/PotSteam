Xi Hu Cu Yu — West Lake Fish in Vinegar Sauce

China | Servings 2 | Prep Time: | Cook Time: 15 minutes | Total Time:
Recipes/China/Xi Hu Cu Yu

Xi Hu Cu Yu

West Lake Fish in Vinegar Sauce

Zhejiang, ChinaRev 3 · view history

Xi Hu Cu Yu, West Lake vinegar fish, is Hangzhou's best-known Zhejiang dish: a whole grass carp is scored and poached in barely moving water with ginger and scallion, then glazed with a glossy sauce of black vinegar, sugar and soy thickened with cornstarch. No oil is used, and the sweet-sour sauce is said to give the carp a flavour close to crab.

Ingredients

2 servings

  • or: Snapper · Seabass · White fish (fillets are easier to handle, though the dish is traditionally served whole)

  • or: Cider vinegar

  • or: Water · Sherry

Instructions

  1. 1
    Score the fish
    Rinse the cleaned carp under cold water and pat it dry. Slash each side two or three times on the diagonal, cutting down into the flesh, so the thick middle cooks at the same pace as the tail.
  2. 2
    Heat the poaching water
    Pour the water into the wok, add the ginger slices and scallion pieces, and bring it to the boil over high heat.
  3. 3
    Poach the fish gently⏱10 minutes
    Lower the fish into the water and turn the heat right down; if the water starts to bubble, splash in a little cold water to calm it. Let the fish sit in the barely trembling water for 8 to 10 minutes, until the fins stand up and the thickest flesh flakes away from the bone.
  4. 4
    Lift onto the platter
    Using two slotted spoons, ease the fish out in one piece onto a warm platter and blot off any water. Pour away all but about 120 ml (1/2 cup) of the poaching liquid and fish out the ginger and scallion.
  5. 5
    Make the slurry
    In a small bowl, stir the cornstarch into the rice wine until no dry lumps remain.
  6. 6
    Build the sauce
    Add the sugar, black vinegar, soy sauce and a pinch of white pepper to the liquid left in the wok and bring it to the boil over medium heat, stirring until the sugar has dissolved.
  7. 7
    Thicken and pour
    Stir the slurry again and whisk in just enough of it to thicken the sauce lightly; stop once it turns glossy and coats a spoon, since the glaze should be thick but not plentiful. Take it off the heat, season with salt, add a few drops of sesame oil if you like, and pour it over the fish. Serve at once, letting diners lift pieces from the platter with chopsticks.

💡 Pro Tips

  • ✓In Hangzhou the live carp is kept in clean water without food for one to two days before cooking, which rids the flesh of its muddy taste.
  • ✓Keep the poaching water from boiling hard and do not leave the fish in it too long; it should stay whole and tender rather than falling apart.
  • ✓Serve the fish whole for Chinese New Year, when a whole fish on the table is considered lucky.

✨ Twist Ideas

Inspiration for your own version of this recipe

  • Steamed: one family version scatters ginger strips over the scored fish, pours on a spoonful of Shaoxing wine and steams it for 10 to 12 minutes, using stock as the sauce base and topping the glazed fish with green onion strips.
  • Marinate the fish briefly in a little soy sauce and rice wine before poaching.
  • Stir a little minced garlic into the sauce as it boils.
  • Use chicken stock instead of the poaching water for the sauce base.
