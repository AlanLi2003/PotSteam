Bok Choy and Shiitake Mushroom Stir-Fry

Cuisine

Chinese

Category

Vegetarian

Combine shiitake mushrooms and bok choy in a garlic sesame sauce for a healthy vegetarian stir-fry.

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp canola oil
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tspn minced or grated fresh ginger
  • 1 cup sliced shiitake mushrooms
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce (tamari)
  • 1 head bok choy, chopped, or 2 to 3 baby bok choy, if you prefer
  • 5 to 6 spring onions, sliced
  • ¼ cup vegetable broth
  • 2 tspn sesame oil
  • 2 tspn sesame seeds, optional

Directions

  1. Heat the oil over medium-high heat in a wok or large skillet. Add the garlic and ginger and stir fry for 30 seconds. Add the mushrooms and cook for 2 to 3 mins.

  2. Add in the soy sauce, the bok choy and spring onions, and cook for a couple more minutes.

  3. Reduce heat to medium-low and add the vegetable broth. Simmer until the bok choy is crisp-tender, 3 to 5 mins.

  4. Stir in the sesame oil and the optional sesame seeds and remove from heat. Taste and add more soy sauce, if desired. Enjoy!
