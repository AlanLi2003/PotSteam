Red-Braised Chicken Wings (红烧鸡翅)

红烧鸡翅

Red-Braised Chicken Wings (红烧鸡翅) — a Chinese recipe made with chicken wings, ginger, ready in about 35 minutes. Step-by-step instructions with photos.

Total time: 35 min Yield: 2 servings Cuisine: Home-style Category: Chinese home cooking Comfort FoodQuick & EasyChinese Chicken

Ingredients

  • chicken wings, halved at joint
  • ginger, sliced
  • scallions, cut into 3 cm sections
  • light soy sauce
  • dark soy sauce
  • Shaoxing wine
  • rock sugar
  • neutral oil

Instructions

  1. Score wings on both sides. Blanch in boiling water 2 minutes. Remove and pat dry.
  2. Heat oil medium. Sear wings 3 minutes per side until golden.
  3. Add ginger, scallions, stir 30 seconds until fragrant.
  4. Add soy sauces, Shaoxing, sugar, and 1 cup warm water. Bring to boil. (Liquid dark and uniform)
  5. Cover, reduce to low. Simmer 20 minutes.
  6. Uncover, raise heat to medium-high. Reduce sauce 5 minutes until glossy and coating.
