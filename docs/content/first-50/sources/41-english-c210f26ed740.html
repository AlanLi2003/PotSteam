    Yunnan Steam-Pot Chicken

    雲南汽鍋雞

    Total time
    120 min
    Difficulty
    Medium
    Servings
    4 servings

    Recipe overview

    A central chimney admits steam that condenses into broth, so no water is added inside the steam pot. Chicken and ginger give a clean flavour; an ordinary sealed bowl is not equivalent.

    01

    Ingredients

    Main ingredients

    • 800 g Bone-in chicken pieces
    • 20 g Ginger
    • 20 g Spring onions
    • To maker level, lower pot only Water for the lower pot

    Chicken seasoning

    • 10 ml Shaoxing wine
    • 3 g Salt
    • 0.5 g White pepper

    02

    Tools

    • Central-chimney ceramic steam pot with matching lid
    • Lower water pot fitting the steam-pot base securely
    • Knife and board
    • Oven gloves, hot-water kettle and thermometer
    • kitchen paper
    • digital kitchen scale (Resolution 0.1 g for 0.5 g pepper.)
    • measuring jug
    • trivet
    • ladle
    • serving bowls

    03

    Steps

    Step 1

    Inspect the steam pot

    Check for cracks and a clear central chimney. Ensure the base fits the lower pot securely, following the maker's instructions.

    Step 2

    Prepare chicken

    Use completely thawed chicken kept at ≤4°C. If still frozen, thaw in the refrigerator before starting; allow additional time. Cut about 4 cm, remove loose bone and excess fat, and pat dry with paper without washing raw chicken.

    Step 3

    Cut aromatics

    Wash ginger and all 20 g spring onions. Slice ginger thinly, cut whites into long sections and greens into short pieces; cover and reserve greens for the finish.

    Step 4

    Season chicken

    Mix chicken with wine and salt, refrigerate 10 minutes and prepare hot water for the lower pot.

    Step 5

    Fill the steam pot

    Put ginger and onion whites in the steam pot, then chicken, leaving space around the central chimney.

    Step 6

    Leave the interior water-free

    Add the marinade and matching lid. Do not pour water inside; incoming steam will form the broth.

    Step 7

    Prepare lower water pot

    During the 10-minute marinade in 04, fill and heat the lower pot to the maker level; now confirm boiling. Follow the ceramic maker instructions for its starting temperature and seating; never put refrigerator-cold ceramic over boiling water or seal the joint yourself. No water inside the chicken pot.

    Step 8

    Seat the steam pot

    Temporarily turn off the heat. With two dry thick mitts, seat the pot already covered in 06 securely and low over its matching lower pot. Restart according to maker instructions until steam is steady; never block vents.

    Step 9

    Steam steadily

    Maintain steady steam for about 90 minutes. Avoid repeatedly opening the inner lid, which loses steam and broth yield.

    Step 10

    Maintain lower water

    Check the lower water level every 20–30 minutes and replenish with boiling water safely. Avoid dry heating and cold splashes on ceramic.

    Step 11

    Check chicken

    Open the lid away from you with dry thick mitts. Probe the thickest chicken centre away from bone at 75°C for 30 seconds. If short, steam about 10 minutes and recheck;10 minutes is a retry interval, not a guarantee of doneness, so extend the actual time as needed.

    Step 12

    Finish and serve

    Add white pepper and onion greens; remove whites. Set the hot pot on a trivet with gloves and portion chicken with the condensed broth.

    Common mistakes

    1. An ordinary sealed bowl lacks the steam inlet needed for this broth.
    2. Filling the inner pot with water changes the steam-pot method.
    3. Blocking the chimney prevents proper steam condensation.
    4. Dry lower pots and thermal shock can damage the cookware.

    Tips

    Use a real steam pot with its compatible lower vessel; wrapping an ordinary bowl tightly is not a substitute. Broth yield depends on steam flow, fit and duration. Ninety-five minutes is a timing guide, not a promised volume; if broth is scarce, check the steam path. Continue steaming until the chicken passes its doneness check.
