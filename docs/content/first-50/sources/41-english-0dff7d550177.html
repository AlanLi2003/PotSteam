Yunnan Recipe

Yunnan Steam Pot Chicken

Yunnan · Serves 3–4 · 2 hours

A clear Yunnan chicken soup made by steaming chicken with ginger and mushrooms.

Recipe at a glance

Serves / makesServes 3–4
Time2 hours
Cuisine or formatYunnan
UseHome cooking and menu literacy

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb bone-in chicken pieces
  • 4 slices ginger
  • 2 scallions
  • 1 cup mushrooms
  • 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine, optional
  • Salt
  • White pepper

Method

  1. Place chicken, ginger, scallions, mushrooms, wine, and salt in a heatproof bowl.
  2. Set over simmering water.
  3. Steam gently for 90 minutes to 2 hours.
  4. Check water below the steamer as needed.
  5. Season the broth with salt and white pepper.
  6. Serve chicken and broth together.

Menu-literacy notes

  • Steam pot chicken is associated with Yunnan.
  • Mushrooms reinforce the Yunnan signal.
  • The flavor should be clean rather than aggressively seasoned.

Variations and substitutions

  • Adjust salt, sugar, chile, and vinegar gradually rather than changing all seasonings at once.
  • For allergies or religious dietary needs, check sauces, wrappers, broth, cooking wine, and shared equipment.
  • When adapting the dish, preserve the core technique and flavor structure that make it recognizable on a menu.
